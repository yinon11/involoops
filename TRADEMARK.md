# Trademark and brand

The **code** in this repository is licensed under its open-source licence (see
`LICENSE`). This file is about something the licence deliberately does not
grant: the name and the brand.

Copyright and trademark are separate rights. An open-source licence gives you
broad rights to the source code. It gives you **no** right to use the project's
name, logo or other brand features. AGPL-3.0 §7(e) expressly permits declining
to grant trademark rights, and that is what this file does.

## Not licensed

- The name **InvoLoops**, and confusingly similar variations.
- The InvoLoops logo and wordmark, and the brand assets under `public/brand/`.

## If you fork or redistribute

You may use every line of the code, subject to `LICENSE`. You must:

- **Rebrand.** Remove the InvoLoops name and logo from the application, its
  output and its documentation, and use your own.
- **Keep the copyright notices.** The licence requires preserving existing
  copyright and licence notices and stating that you modified the work. Removing
  attribution is a licence violation, separate from any trademark question.
- **Not imply endorsement.** Do not present your fork as official, endorsed by,
  or affiliated with InvoLoops.

There is a practical reason beyond trademark: the brand also appears in
generated output, including the digital-signature line on PDFs and the
software-vendor identifier in the uniform-structure (מבנה אחיד) export the Israel
Tax Authority reads. A fork must change those so its documents identify **its**
software, not ours.

## What you may always do

Referential use is fine, and welcome:

- saying your project is "based on InvoLoops" or "a fork of InvoLoops";
- naming InvoLoops in comparisons, documentation, articles or talks;
- linking to this repository.

That is nominative use — describing what something is. It is not what this file
restricts.
